Why Delivery Routing Is Hard

  • Last-mile delivery involves visiting multiple stops and returning to the starting point efficiently.
  • As the stop count N increases, the number of candidate routes grows rapidly.
  • Exact shortest-route guarantees can become too slow for practical batch sizes.

Project Focus

  • Compare exact algorithms against heuristic and local-search approaches on real coordinates.
  • Measure runtime scaling across small, medium, large, and extra-large stop tiers.
