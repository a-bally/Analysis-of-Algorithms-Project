Exact Algorithms

  • Brute Force: Evaluates permutations of stops to guarantee the minimum-distance tour.
  • Dynamic Programming: Uses memoized subproblems (bitmask states) to find an exact tour without re-evaluating identical states.

Heuristic & Local Search Algorithms

  • Greedy Nearest Neighbor: Starts at stop 0 and repeatedly picks the closest unvisited stop before returning to the start.
  • 2-Opt: Starts from an initial route (such as Greedy) and swaps pairs of edges when doing so shortens the total tour.
